About

Welcome! I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Universität Mannheim, Germany.

Job Market Paper

Machine-Learning-Assisted Empirical Bayes Estimation for Regression on Grouped Network Data
[Available upon Request]

We propose a two-way effects model for matched data represented by a bipartite network with latent group structure. A directed acyclic graph (DAG) encodes the assumed relationships among the observed outcomes, network links, latent effects, group memberships, and prior parameters, thereby clarifying the data-generating process and organizing the empirical Bayes (EB) estimation procedure. Unobserved heterogeneity is modeled using group-specific prior distributions, allowing individual effects to vary both across and within groups. Although the two-way effects can be estimated by least-squares estimator, LS may be imprecise when the underlying bipartite network is sparse and connected across latent groups. We therefore develop a machine-learning-assisted EB estimator that first learns the latent group structure from the network and then exploits this structure to shrink the estimated effects toward their corresponding group-specific distributions. We establish the theoretical properties of the proposed estimator, and Monte Carlo simulations show that it substantially reduces estimation error relative to OLS under the data-generating processes considered. Finally, in an empirical application to Amazon user--product ratings, we use the proposed method to estimate and characterize heterogeneity in the user- and product-specific components of ratings.
